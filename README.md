# SimMate

A free driving coach for Assetto Corsa Competizione, in Thai and English.

SimMate records every lap you drive, works out where the time goes, and tells you what to change in the words
drivers use: *brake at the 150 board, not the 200*, *soften the front bar one click*. Every piece of advice shows
the rule and the numbers behind it.

**Website:** https://simmate.phu-space.com · **ภาษาไทย:** เปิดเว็บแล้วกด "ไทย" มุมขวาบน

## Download

Free on the Microsoft Store: https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9MXSCNMJ6MX2. Windows 10 or 11 and ACC are all you
need; Python and the browser engine come inside, and the Store keeps SimMate up to date.

## Privacy

Your laps stay on your PC. SimMate can share app errors, which tracks you drive and your fastest laps with the
developer, but only the parts you switch on in Settings, and every switch starts off. What each one sends:
https://simmate.phu-space.com/privacy

## Reporting a problem / แจ้งปัญหา

Something broke, froze or gave odd advice: open a [problem report](../../issues/new?template=bug.yml). Want
SimMate to do something new: [suggest an idea](../../issues/new?template=idea.yml). Both forms take Thai or
English. For a problem, attach `archiver.log` from `Documents\Assetto Corsa Competizione\SimMate data`.

เจอปัญหา แอปค้าง หรือคำแนะนำแปลก ๆ กด[แจ้งปัญหา](../../issues/new?template=bug.yml) อยากได้อะไรเพิ่ม
กด[เสนอไอเดีย](../../issues/new?template=idea.yml) เขียนไทยหรืออังกฤษก็ได้ ถ้าแจ้งปัญหา ช่วยแนบไฟล์
`archiver.log` จากโฟลเดอร์ `Documents\Assetto Corsa Competizione\SimMate data` มาด้วยนะครับ

This repository holds problem reports, ideas and release notes. The source is kept private.
